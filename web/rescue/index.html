Mission: bring all 5 survivors (red SOS beacons) to the rescue camp. Stop next to their house for 2 s to load (2 at a time), then drive into the green camp ring. The experimenter picks the disaster: flood, earthquake, wildfire or landslide.
Quest controllers Point the right laser at the map and pull trigger = send the rover · hold grip = drive it yourself (left stick throttle, right stick turn) · A arm · B cancel route · Y stop · X switch view (cockpit / table) · click left stick = re-centre. In VR you start in the cockpit: you see through the rover's front camera, with a map tablet on the dashboard to point at.
Keyboard and mouse (desktop testing) Click the map = send the rover · right-drag = orbit · wheel = zoom · hold Space + W/S/A/D = drive · R arm · T disarm · X stop · C cancel route · V cockpit / table view
